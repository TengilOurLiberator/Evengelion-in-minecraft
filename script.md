# Angel Attack: the script

The first episode's story, told in new words. You are the boy, Shinji Ikari, fourteen, and you have no
recorded lines: when someone speaks to you, you answer aloud in your own words.

Four people speak, and the base itself:

| Part | Who | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| **MISATO** | Captain Misato Katsuragi, 29, operations | Bright and quick, smiling through most of it. Warm, a little rough. Drops the smile entirely in scene 5. |
| **RITSUKO** | Dr. Ritsuko Akagi, 30, head of the project | Level, dry, exact. Never hurries, never raises her voice until 5.33. |
| **GENDO** | Commander Gendo Ikari, 48, your father | Low and slow. Almost no inflection. He does not persuade; he states. Leave silence around his lines. |
| **REI** | Rei Ayanami, 14, the other pilot, badly hurt | Very soft, close to the microphone, flat. Each line costs her breath. |
| **BASE** | The base computer and public address | Even, unhurried, no feeling at all. The same pace whether it is a door closing or the city falling. |

How to read the page:

- A spoken line is a name, a number, and the words. Read only the words.
- *(Italics in brackets after a line)* say where the voice comes from or how it is said. Do not read them.
- `> ` lines are what happens in the world.
- **▶ WAIT** means the story stops until you, as the boy, say something. Whoever spoke last answers you
  live, in character, and then the script goes on. The lines after a wait are written to follow any answer.
- **▶ WAIT FOR YES** stops until you agree to pilot.

When recording, read one character at a time, top to bottom, from the sheets in `recording/`. Leave two
seconds of silence between lines. If you fluff a line, just say it again; the last take is the one kept.

## Scene 1. The empty city

> Midsummer, noon, cicadas. You are standing on a railway platform beside a train that is not going anywhere.
> No one is here. In your pocket: your father's letter, one word long, and a photograph of a woman with a
> note on the back: *I'll come for you at the station. Wait there. M.K.*
> Outside the station is a payphone. You pick it up.

**BASE** `1.01` This is an automated notice. A state of emergency has been declared for the entire Tokai region. All civilian lines are closed until further notice. *(telephone)*

**BASE** `1.02` Residents are to go to their assigned shelter at once. This message will repeat. *(telephone)*

> You walk out into the street. Far down the road a girl in a school uniform is standing perfectly still,
> looking at you. Birds go up from the wires. When you look again she is not there.
> The ground jumps. Between the hills, something as tall as the hills walks out of the sea.

**BASE** `1.03` Attention. A hostile object has come ashore and is moving inland. Anyone still above ground, get below. Now. *(street loudspeakers)*

> Aircraft circle it and fire. It does not notice. One of them comes down burning thirty metres from you.
> A blue car slides sideways out of the smoke and stops with its door open.

**MISATO** `1.04` Shinji Ikari? That's you. Get in!

**MISATO** `1.05` Sorry. I meant to be here before that was.

> If you stand there:

**MISATO** `1.06` Now, please! I'll introduce myself when we're not about to be stepped on.

## Scene 2. The road

> She drives fast and badly, out of town and up into the hills.

**MISATO** `2.01` Misato Katsuragi. Captain, on paper, but if you call me that I'll put you out at the next corner. Misato.

**MISATO** `2.02` Are you hurt? Anything bleeding that I ought to know about?

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `2.03` Good. That thing behind us, we call an Angel. Don't ask me who chose the name.

**MISATO** `2.04` The army has thrown everything it owns at it since dawn. You saw how much it minded.

> She stops on a ridge above the town and looks back through field glasses.

**MISATO** `2.05` Wait. Why are they pulling the planes back?

**MISATO** `2.06` Oh, no. They wouldn't. Not this close to... Down! Head down, right now!

> White. Then the sound. The car goes over twice and ends on its side.

**MISATO** `2.07` Still with me? Say something.

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `2.08` Me too. Mouthful of sand, that's all. Come on. Back against the roof, and push when I say.

**MISATO** `2.09` One. Two. Three!

> You push. The car drops back onto its wheels.

**MISATO** `2.10` There. Thank you. You're stronger than you look, Shinji.

**MISATO** `2.11` Look at her. I had eleven payments left on this car.

> She drives on with the windscreen cracked, a telephone against her shoulder.

**MISATO** `2.12` Ritsuko, it's me. I have him. Yes, in one piece, what do you take me for? Have a car train waiting. The fast one.

**RITSUKO** `2.13` You're forty minutes late. The mine didn't kill it, Misato. It is standing in the crater growing back what it lost. *(telephone)*

**RITSUKO** `2.14` It will be over the city inside the hour. Bring him straight down. *(telephone)*

**BASE** `2.15` All stations. United Nations forces have withdrawn. Command of this operation passes to NERV, effective now. *(car radio)*

**MISATO** `2.16` Hear that? That's every general in the country admitting he has no more ideas. So now it's ours.

## Scene 3. The descent

> A blast door in a hillside. The car rolls onto a flat rail wagon, and the wagon starts down into the dark.

**BASE** `3.01` Gate closing. Car train departing for headquarters. Please remain in your vehicle. *(public address)*

**MISATO** `3.02` Did your father send you a pass? A card, with your face on it?

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `3.03` Keep hold of it. And this is yours. Read it on the way down; there's a test.

> She drops a booklet in your lap. On the cover: *NERV. For Your Eyes Only.*

**MISATO** `3.04` There isn't a test. NERV. A special agency under the United Nations, answering to nobody you'll ever get to vote for. Your father runs it.

**MISATO** `3.05` Do you know what he does here?

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `3.06` Mm. He isn't an easy man to know. I gather the two of you aren't close.

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `3.07` Then we have that in common. I couldn't stand mine either.

> The tunnel wall falls away.

**MISATO** `3.08` Ah. Here. Look out of your window.

**MISATO** `3.09` The Geofront. A whole hollow country under the city, and our headquarters at the bottom of it.

**MISATO** `3.10` This is where the world gets put back together, if it does. People's last fortress. Not bad, is it?

## Scene 4. Lost

> Headquarters. White corridors, moving walkways, doors with numbers. You follow her.

**MISATO** `4.01` This way. I think it's this way.

**MISATO** `4.02` Don't look at me like that. I've only been posted here a little while, and whoever built this place had a grudge.

> You pass a door you have certainly passed before.

**MISATO** `4.03` We've been past that door already, haven't we.

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `4.04` Well. That's what the system is for. If you can't find a thing, make it come and find you.

**BASE** `4.05` Paging Doctor Ritsuko Akagi, Technical Division One. Doctor Akagi, please contact Captain Katsuragi of Operations at once. *(public address)*

> A lift opens. A woman in a lab coat over a swimsuit, hair still wet, steps out.

**RITSUKO** `4.06` Captain Katsuragi. We have no time and no people to spare, and you are wasting both.

**MISATO** `4.07` Sorry! Really. Sorry.

**RITSUKO** `4.08` So this is the boy.

**MISATO** `4.09` The one the Marduk report picked. Third Child, on paper.

**RITSUKO** `4.10` Ritsuko Akagi. I run the Evangelion project. I'm glad you came, Shinji.

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `4.11` Don't take it to heart. He comes by the cold shoulder honestly; you've met his father.

**BASE** `4.12` All personnel, first stage alert. Prepare for ground interception. Repeat: prepare for ground interception. *(public address)*

**MISATO** `4.13` That's it, then. How is Unit One?

**RITSUKO** `4.14` Type B equipment, sitting in coolant. As ready as it can be made.

**MISATO** `4.15` Does it actually run? It has never once started.

**RITSUKO** `4.16` The chance of activation is about one in a billion. In my division we call that "not zero."

**MISATO** `4.17` That's a number people say when they mean "no."

**RITSUKO** `4.18` It's a number I say when I mean "we have nothing else." Mind your step. It's dark through here.

## Scene 5. The cage

> A raft across a lake of something red, in the dark. A door seals behind you. You are on a narrow bridge
> and cannot see the far end of it.

**RITSUKO** `5.01` Stand there. Don't move until the lights come up.

> The lights come up. Six metres in front of you is a face the size of a house, horned, purple, looking down at you.

**RITSUKO** `5.02` This is what people built when they ran out of other answers. Not a robot. A made human being, the height of a tower.

**RITSUKO** `5.03` Evangelion. This is the first of the true series, Unit Zero-One. It was built here, in secret, and it is the last card we hold.

**▶ WAIT**

**GENDO** `5.04` Shinji. *(from the booth, high above)*

**GENDO** `5.05` Three years. You came when you were sent for. Good. *(from above)*

**▶ WAIT**

**GENDO** `5.06` Prepare to launch. *(from above)*

**MISATO** `5.07` Launch? Unit Zero is still frozen. Wait. You mean to send Unit One.

**RITSUKO** `5.08` There is no other way left.

**MISATO** `5.09` Rei can't even stand yet! We have no pilot.

**RITSUKO** `5.10` One was delivered a few minutes ago.

**MISATO** `5.11` You are not serious.

**RITSUKO** `5.12` Shinji Ikari. You will be the one inside it.

**▶ WAIT**

**MISATO** `5.13` It took Rei seven months just to link with hers. He stepped off a train this morning!

**RITSUKO** `5.14` Nobody is asking him to fight well. We need someone in that seat whom the unit will accept.

**MISATO** `5.15` But...

**RITSUKO** `5.16` The Angel is what matters today. If someone has any chance of linking with an Eva, we use them. You would give the same order, Captain.

**MISATO** `5.17` Yes. I would.

**GENDO** `5.18` You were sent for because I have a use for you. There is no other reason. *(from above)*

**GENDO** `5.19` No one else alive can do this. Get in. *(from above)*

**▶ WAIT**

**GENDO** `5.20` Then choose quickly. Sit in it, or go back where you came from. Standing there you are only in the way. *(from above)*

> The whole room shakes. Dust comes down through the lights.

**BASE** `5.21` Impact, surface level. The Angel is directly above headquarters. *(public address)*

**GENDO** `5.22` It has found us. *(from above)*

**RITSUKO** `5.23` Shinji. There is no time.

**MISATO** `5.24` What did you come all this way for? Look at him. Look at it. Then decide, but decide facing them.

**▶ WAIT** *(If you agree here, the story goes straight to 5.42.)*

**GENDO** `5.25` Medical. Wake Rei. *(from above, into a handset)*

**GENDO** `5.26` She is not dead. She will serve. *(from above)*

**GENDO** `5.27` Rei. The replacement has refused. It falls to you again. *(from above, into a handset)*

**REI** `5.28` Yes. *(intercom)*

**RITSUKO** `5.29` Rewrite Unit One's core for Rei. Start the sequence again!

**BASE** `5.30` Current operation halted. Beginning system restart, Unit Zero-One. *(public address)*

> A side door opens. Two medics wheel in a hospital bed. On it, a girl your own age, bandaged over one eye,
> one arm in a cast, a drip in the other. The girl from the street. She tries to sit up.

**REI** `5.31` *(No words. One long breath in through the teeth, held, and let go shaking.)*

> The room is hit again, harder. A lighting rig tears loose from the ceiling above you. The girl is thrown
> from the bed. A hand the size of a bus comes up out of the red lake and stops over your head. The steel
> breaks on it.

**BASE** `5.32` Warning. Unit Zero-One, right arm in motion. Restraints broken. No pilot aboard. *(public address)*

**RITSUKO** `5.33` It moved? It can't have. The plug isn't even in!

**MISATO** `5.34` Nobody told it to. It put its hand over him.

**MISATO** `5.35` He can do this.

> You go to the girl on the floor and lift her. Your hand comes away red.

**REI** `5.36` I'm all right. I can go.

**REI** `5.37` Let go, please. They need me to stand up.

**REI** `5.38` It's what I'm for.

**▶ WAIT FOR YES**

> While you say nothing, these come, some seconds apart:

**REI** `5.39` Don't look like that. It only hurts.

**BASE** `5.40` Armor layer eighteen breached. Estimated time to headquarters: nine minutes. *(public address)*

**GENDO** `5.41` Decide. *(from above)*

> When you say you will do it:

**MISATO** `5.42` Thank you, Shinji.

**RITSUKO** `5.43` Good. Come with me. I'll tell you only what you need; there is no time for the rest.

> Your father looks down from the glass and says nothing at all. As they lead you out, behind you:

**REI** `5.44` Why?

## Scene 6. The plug

> A white tube barely wider than your shoulders. A seat, two grips. The hatch closes and it is dark.

**BASE** `6.01` Cooling complete. Cage set to launch position. *(public address, heard through the hull)*

**BASE** `6.02` Entry plug inserted. Plug locked. *(cockpit)*

**RITSUKO** `6.03` Shinji, can you hear me? Don't touch anything yet. *(radio)*

**BASE** `6.04` Flooding the plug. *(cockpit)*

> Something warm and amber rises over your feet, your knees, your chest.

**RITSUKO** `6.05` Don't fight it. Breathe it in. Once it fills your lungs it carries the air for you. *(radio)*

**MISATO** `6.06` It's foul, I know. Half a minute and you'll forget it's there. Bear with it. *(radio)*

**BASE** `6.07` Main power connected. Beginning first contact. *(cockpit)*

> The walls of the tube turn to glass, and then to nothing. You are looking at the cage from a great height.

**BASE** `6.08` Nerve links open. Language base set. All primary connections clear. *(cockpit)*

**BASE** `6.09` Synchronization at forty-one point three percent. *(cockpit)*

**RITSUKO** `6.10` Forty-one. With no suit and no training. *(radio)*

**RITSUKO** `6.11` Harmonics all normal. Nothing is running away from us. It works. We can do this. *(radio)*

**MISATO** `6.12` Begin launch preparations! *(radio)*

**BASE** `6.13` First lock bolts released. Bridge moving clear. Second lock bolts released. *(cockpit)*

**BASE** `6.14` All safeties off. Internal batteries charged. External power steady. *(cockpit)*

**BASE** `6.15` Unit Zero-One to launch pad. Route clear. All green. *(cockpit)*

**MISATO** `6.16` Understood. Commander. Do I have your word? *(radio)*

**GENDO** `6.17` You have it. If the Angel is not stopped, there is nothing after today. *(radio)*

**MISATO** `6.18` Shinji. Hold on to something. *(radio)*

**MISATO** `6.19` Evangelion Unit One: launch! *(radio)*

> The floor hits you. The shaft goes past like a train. Then night air, and the city, and at the far end of
> the avenue, it.

**BASE** `6.20` Unit Zero-One on the surface. Final restraints released. *(cockpit)*

**MISATO** `6.21` Shinji. Whatever happens out there, come back. *(radio)*

## Scene 7. The fight

> From here it is yours. These are said when the thing they describe happens.

> At the start:

**MISATO** `7.01` All right. Don't think about fighting yet. Just think about walking. *(radio)*

**RITSUKO** `7.02` It moves when you mean it to. Don't push at the controls. Want the step. *(radio)*

> Your first steps:

**MISATO** `7.03` That's it! You're walking! *(radio)*

> The first time you are hit:

**MISATO** `7.04` Shinji! That isn't your arm, it only feels like it. Stay with me! *(radio)*

**RITSUKO** `7.05` The pain comes back through the link. It is real to you and nothing of yours is broken. Keep moving. *(radio)*

> Your first blow stops short on a wall of light:

**RITSUKO** `7.06` That light is its barrier. While it holds, nothing reaches it. *(radio)*

**RITSUKO** `7.07` Get close. Your unit throws a field of the same kind. At arm's length they cancel. *(radio)*

**MISATO** `7.08` The red sphere in its chest! That's the heart of it. Hit there and nowhere else. *(radio)*

> Its eyes begin to glow:

**MISATO** `7.09` Its eyes! Move! Put a building between you! *(radio)*

> You are nearly beaten:

**BASE** `7.10` Warning. Head armor damaged. Pilot vital signs unstable. *(cockpit)*

**MISATO** `7.11` Get up. Shinji, get up! It isn't finished, and neither are you. *(radio)*

> The sphere cracks:

**RITSUKO** `7.12` The core is cracking. Again. The same place. *(radio)*

**MISATO** `7.13` Now! Everything you have! *(radio)*

> Dying, it wraps itself around you:

**RITSUKO** `7.14` It's folding itself around the unit. It means to take you with it. Break loose! *(radio)*

> If you win:

**BASE** `7.15` Target silent. Pattern blue is gone. The Angel is destroyed. *(cockpit)*

**MISATO** `7.16` He did it. Shinji, you did it. Stay where you are. I'm coming up to get you. *(radio)*

**RITSUKO** `7.17` One in a billion. *(radio)*

**GENDO** `7.18` Recover the unit. *(radio)*

> If you fall:

**BASE** `7.19` Unit Zero-One silent. Pilot status unknown. *(cockpit)*

**MISATO** `7.20` Shinji! Answer me! Pull him back. Pull him back, and send him up again. *(radio)*

> After the fight the base is open. Everyone is where you would expect to find them, and will talk.
