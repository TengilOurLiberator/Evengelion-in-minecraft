# BASE

**Voice:** the base computer / PA voice, monotone.  **Lines:** 24

**How to record**

- Read in your own natural voice, at your own pitch. Do not try to sound like the character: the voice is changed afterwards. Put the *feeling* in (the rushing, the dryness, the quiet); leave the voice alone.
- Read every line top to bottom, in order. Leave a clear pause of about two seconds after each line.
- If you flub a line, stop, wait the two seconds, and read it again from its beginning. The last good take is the one that is kept. Do not read the line number, the scene, or the small print under a line.
- Read what is written. The text in (brackets) after a line is how it is heard in the game, not something to say.
